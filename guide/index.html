For teachers and organisers

List your milonga or class for free

Being listed is free, for everyone, so dancers can trust the guide is complete.

Send it by WhatsApp

  • Message the guide the details, or forward your Facebook or Instagram post. It reads the name, date, time and place, shows you how it will look, and lists it after your yes.
  • Weekly events are listed every week until a date you choose.
  • Changes the same way: "We've moved to the Crypt tonight" or "the DJ is Ana", and the listing changes at once, for dancers and for the AI assistants that read the guide.
  • The guide tells you how many dancers asked about each of your events.

Members come first

Members are teachers and organisers who pay for Tango Coordinator, their own WhatsApp assistant. Their listings update themselves: the guide listens to their assistant, so a change they send it is here within a minute. Dancers who choose them are passed straight to their assistant to book, and their events are shown first each day, labelled "★ Featured". Everyone else is still listed, underneath, with a link to their own page.

Keeping it right

Listings you can trust

1

A local person checks each city

Before a city goes live, someone who dances there looks after its listings: they check new organisers' events and fix or remove anything wrong.

2

Spotted a mistake? Tell the guide

"That milonga's cancelled tonight." It goes to the organiser and the local checker, without your name or number, and they put it right.

3

It only ever replies

The guide never messages you first: no adverts, no newsletters. You ask, it answers.

Made for dancers

Not a general AI assistant: your city's dance community

1

Just say hello

No app, no account, no password. Tap the button on your phone, or scan the code on a computer, and WhatsApp opens with your first message typed in.

2

Only your first name

It never reads your email, calendar, contacts or other chats, and never asks for a password. Your WhatsApp number and first name are all it needs.

3

Book with the real organiser

Pick something and you're put through to that teacher's or organiser's own assistant, which knows the real places left, the waiting list and tonight's changes, and books you in.

4

Using your own AI assistant?

It can connect to the guide directly (the guide speaks MCP, the standard way AI assistants plug into services) and gets the same up-to-date listings. To book, it sends you here, to the guide on WhatsApp, which puts you through to the organiser's own assistant, under the same rules as everyone.

Privacy

What the guide keeps

If you're a dancer

  • Your WhatsApp number and first name, so it can reply.
  • Your messages, for 7 days, so it can follow the conversation.
  • The last city you asked about, so you aren't asked every time.
  • Which events dancers chose, as a count only, never who.
  • If you tell it a listing is wrong, what you said, for 14 days. The organiser sees what was said, never who said it.
  • If you ask for your profile: the private links to your pages at the schools that know you. Your bookings stay with each school and are read when you open it. Unopened for 180 days, it's deleted.

It passes nothing about you to anyone. If you get in touch with a school or organiser, you send the first message yourself.

If you list events

  • Your number, while you list with the guide, so only you can change your listings.
  • The events you send, deleted 7 days after they happen.
