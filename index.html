Privacy

Tango Coordinator provides a WhatsApp assistant that tango schools use to organise their classes. We keep only what is needed to do that:

  1. the student's WhatsApp number and first name only (we never store surnames),
  2. their bookings and, if they tell us, the city or time zone they live in, and
  3. recent messages, so the assistant can follow the conversation.

If you ask for reminders and updates, we keep your number and first name so we can send them, until you reply STOP. Messages are deleted after 7 days, and bookings 90 days after the lesson. A weekly backup of bookings is kept for up to a month. Attendance pages show first names only, never phone numbers. We do not sell data, show adverts or use tracking. Messages and bookings are processed by our service providers only to provide the service: Meta (WhatsApp), Twilio (WhatsApp, during testing), Anthropic (the AI that reads the messages), Render (hosting, EU) and Neon (database). Each school is responsible for its own students' data; we process it on the school's behalf.

To see or delete your data, email nick.wild3@gmail.com.
